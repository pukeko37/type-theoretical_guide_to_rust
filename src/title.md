# A Type-Theoretical Guide to Rust

*What type theory reveals about Rust's design*

</div>

<div class="title-blurb">

Rust's type system is not just a safety net — it is a design tool. This book develops a formal model that reveals the structure behind traits, bounds, lifetimes, and generics, showing why they were designed the way they were and how they fit together as a coherent whole. Understanding that structure changes the way you write Rust: from working around the compiler to designing with it, and from reaching for familiar patterns to exploring the full reach of what the type system makes possible.

</div>
